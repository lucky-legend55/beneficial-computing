Time Space Compression

What is Time Space Compression

Time space compression is the geographic concept that describe how increased globalisation can occur through enhancements in communication technology creating a world that feels increasingly interconnected.

Positive Contributions to Society

  • reduces time needed to share ideas
  • allows for people to share expieriences of events first hand
  • creates a greater sense of global understanding as people are able to share cultural ideas and norms without travelling

Reducing time constraints to sharing ideas

With the development of technology people across the globe are able to communicate in real time. Communicating without time constraints allows for new ideas to be collaborated on and improved at speed allowing for greater advances in a variety of fields.

First hand experiences and accounts

Advancing technology also provides people a platform to share their own experiences with the global community. This is particularly beneficial to society as it allows for real accounts of global news and events without bias from large corporations and reporting bodies. This was particularly visible during the 2025 uprising against the Nepali Government that was largely documented online by not only news corporations but also a British tourist who found himself in the action.

Global norms and understanding

Technological advances also serve to improve global understnading as people are able to share beliefs and traditions. Sharing these globally helps to normalise different cultural practices throughout different countries and creates a culture of greater understanding and tolerance.
