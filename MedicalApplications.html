Medical applications of computing

One of the fields that relies most heavily on technology to save lives and improves the quality of life of many people is the medical field. Beneficial applications include:

  1. Hospital information systems
    Hospitals are increasingly using computer information networks in order to better organise and manage information to do with patients, research and education. This is known as Medical Informatics. A central hospital system can make use of servers to store patient information to allow staff easier/ remote access to patient data and makes it easier to create centralised systems for protecting patient data.
  2. Laboratory Computing
    Labs provide analysis of bloods, microbiology, genes, amongst other things. Access to patient databases would help with organisation and getting quick results for patients.
