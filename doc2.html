Roles in CYBERSECURITY

Cybersecurity is a rapidly growing field that encompasses a wide range of roles and responsibilities. Some of the key roles in cybersecurity include:

  • Security Analyst: Responsible for monitoring and analyzing security threats and vulnerabilities.
  • Penetration Tester: Conducts simulated attacks to identify weaknesses in systems and networks.
  • Security Engineer: Designs and implements security measures to protect systems and data.
  • Incident Responder: Responds to security incidents and works to mitigate damage.
  • Security Consultant: Provides expert advice on security best practices and risk management.
